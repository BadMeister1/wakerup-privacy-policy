This Privacy Policy explains how WakerUp ("the App", "we", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data transparently.

1. Data Controller

The data controller responsible for your personal data is:

PepaAppStudio
Email: pepaappstudio.@gmail.com

2. What Data We Collect

The App itself does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. However, the third-party services we use (described below) may collect certain data automatically.

2.1 Data Collected Automatically via Firebase

The App uses Firebase services provided by Google LLC. Firebase may automatically collect:

  • Anonymous user identifier — a randomly generated ID used to associate your app data (alarm settings, in-app currency) across devices. This ID is not linked to your real identity unless you choose to sign in with Google.
  • App usage analytics — anonymous data about how you interact with the App (screens visited, features used, crash reports). This data is used solely to improve the App.
  • Device information — device model, operating system version, and language, collected by Firebase Analytics for aggregated reporting.

2.2 Google Sign-In (Optional)

You may optionally sign in with your Google account. If you do, we store your Google account ID and display name in our database to identify your account across devices. We do not access your Google email, contacts, or any other Google data.

2.3 Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO)

The App requests access to your microphone to enable the "noise detection" feature, which allows you to dismiss an alarm by shouting or making a loud sound. Important:

  • Audio is processed locally on your device only in real time.
  • No audio is recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere.
  • No audio data is shared with any third party.
  • Microphone access is used only while an alarm is active and the noise detection feature is enabled.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To store and synchronize your alarm settings and in-app progress across devices.
  • To improve the App based on anonymous usage analytics.
  • To enable alarm dismissal via noise detection (microphone, local processing only).

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sell it to any third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase Authentication — anonymous and Google Sign-In authentication. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Cloud Firestore — cloud database for storing alarm settings and in-app data. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage analytics. Google Privacy Policy

Our Firestore database is located in europe-west3 (Frankfurt, Germany). Some Firebase services may process data in the United States. Such transfers are carried out under Google's Data Processing Terms and Standard Contractual Clauses in accordance with GDPR.

5. Data Retention

  • Anonymous account data (alarm settings, coins, gems) — retained as long as the anonymous session exists on your device. Uninstalling the App removes the anonymous session.
  • Google Sign-In account data — retained until you request deletion.
  • Firebase Analytics data — retained for up to 14 months per Google's standard policy.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — you may request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — you may request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure — you may request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to restriction — you may request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Right to data portability — you may request your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — you may object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to lodge a complaint — you may file a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at pepaappstudio.@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Data Security

All data transmitted between the App and Firebase is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Firebase applies industry-standard security measures for data at rest. We do not store sensitive data (such as passwords) in the App.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
